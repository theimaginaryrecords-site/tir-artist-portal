Frequently Asked Questions
How long does release review take?
Our team reviews all submissions within 3–5 business days. You'll see the status change in your dashboard from Pending → Approved → Live.
What audio format should I upload?
We require WAV or FLAC files at minimum 44.1kHz / 16-bit. MP3 is not accepted. This ensures the best quality on all platforms.
What artwork size is required?
Cover artwork must be exactly 3000×3000 pixels in JPG or PNG format. The image must be square. Text on artwork is allowed but must not contain misleading platform logos.
When do I get paid?
Royalties are collected quarterly and paid to your PayPal once you reach ₹500. Make sure your PayPal email is set correctly in your profile.
