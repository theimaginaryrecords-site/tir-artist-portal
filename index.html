Agreements between you and The Imaginary Records.

Standard Agreement Template

Template

TIR Standard Artist Agreement (template). This is the template TIR uses for artist relationships — your signed copy, if any, appears above.

1. Non-exclusive distribution. The artist grants The Imaginary Records a non-exclusive, worldwide license to distribute, stream and monetize their recordings through TIR's distribution partners. The artist keeps full ownership of masters and publishing at all times.

2. Royalty share. The artist receives the royalty share stated in their signed agreement (standard: 85% of net royalties); TIR retains the remainder as a distribution and services fee.

3. Term. The agreement runs for the term stated in the signed copy (standard: 12 months from the first release), renewing automatically unless either party gives 30 days' written notice.

4. Territory. Worldwide, across all current and future digital platforms served by TIR's distributors.

5. Reporting. Royalty statements are issued monthly in the portal; payouts follow within 45–60 days of each reporting period's close.
